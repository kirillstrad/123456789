Ordinary damage during vulnerable phases reduces lamp without replacing the dash animation; lethal damage cancels it. The ending flare deals no area damage.

The same movement-input mapping drives walking and dash. Body/smoke opacity is applied separately per scene/mask primitive and restored after drawing; fully transparent primitives skip drawing. The reformation adds a temporary local light. Fixed snapshots produce deterministic smoke/sparks; pause advances neither dash nor cooldown.
