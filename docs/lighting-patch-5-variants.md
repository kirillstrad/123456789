# Lighting patch 5: nine placed source variants and lamp contact

The existing test brazier remains first, with its original identity and transform. Eight additional instances cover brazier.b/c, candlesticks.a/b/c and wall-light.a/b/c. All start off. Three wall models are placed along the north wall with yaw 180 so exposed-side approach points face into the nave. No gallery wiring or new model families are included. Existing context sharing, target ownership, six-LAMP policy, payment marker, permanent burning and reset continue to apply. Multi-emitter models ignite as one target and pay once.

Ignition animation frames now carry the source contact point and actor transform. The existing procedural pose is fitted to that point: a bounded deterministic search adjusts crouch and lean, then blends the lamp hand into reach. The lamp's authored flame lies 0.08 below its left-hand anchor. Contact is checked against that flame, not merely a nearby wrist. The fit holds from progress 0.5 through 0.65, including the 0.6 effect marker, and blends back to the original endpoints. No vendor clips, attack or locomotion authoring are rebuilt. Invalid bindings or unreachable contact fail explicitly rather than silently clamping a visibly missed target.

Tests cover model/instance uniqueness, original-brazier compatibility, spawn-to-approach planning for every placement, physical clearance, inward wall approaches, actual vendor lamp emission at contact, pose endpoints, sequential navigation and ignition of all nine instances, one six-unit payment per object, aggregate emitter count and reset. Candles and wall lights keep their authored absence of physical colliders; brazier footprints retain the existing conservative circle conversion.

The existing Chromium test continues to cover the original brazier and is not claimed as browser coverage of all nine placements. The new tests run in the existing full-unit CI workflow. Per-variant Chromium screenshots, visual quality of deep crouches, geometry occlusion, performance and human acceptance remain pending. Wall attachment and source placement are candidate layout, not approved final level design. Door camera and extra scarlet hero details are out of scope. This patch is not merged into main by publication.
